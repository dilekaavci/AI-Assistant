Internships allow students to gain practical experience in a professional working environment.

During an internship, students may apply knowledge and skills learned in their academic courses to real-world tasks and projects.

Students should follow the internship requirements and procedures defined by their academic department.

Internship responsibilities may include completing assigned tasks, communicating with supervisors, and documenting the work performed.

After completing an internship, students may be required to submit documents or reports according to their department's requirements.
